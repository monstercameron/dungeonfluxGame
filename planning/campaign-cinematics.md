# Campaign bookends, critical cues and media continuity

Date: 2026-09-30
Status: Planned F46 and refinements to F15/F20/F35/F44; runtime evidence pending

Reuse `df-narrative` for story significance, `df-knowledge` for permitted facts,
`df-presentation` for pure cue/shot proposals, `df-media` for admitted jobs and
`df-assets` for immutable complete publication. `df-engine`/`df-session` stage and
commit references/results; native effect execution performs all paid I/O. This
adds no cinematic crate or alternate writer. See [Asset engine](asset-engine.md),
[Tempo engine](tempo-engine.md) and [Long-horizon state](long-horizon-state.md).

## Recaps and trailers

`BookendSpec` distinguishes Recap and SpeculativeTrailer, binds campaign/run,
covered decision/event range, audience, policy/content/identity revisions, selected
locale, bounded duration/shot/text/bytes, expiry and approved budget/execution mode.
`FactSelection` holds exact committed event/claim references and observer scope;
`BookendPlan` contains approved narrative/shot/caption/music cues and fallback.
Recaps summarize authorized established events and do not turn false NPC beliefs
into canonical truth or hide their attribution. A newly joined member sees only
permitted knowledge, with a separate observer recap where appropriate. Prior
screenshots/audio require capture rights and cannot bypass current audience checks.

Trailers use already permitted active threads and generic speculative imagery,
clearly labeled as a possible future. They cannot reveal an unknown boss/future
branch through images, music, subtitles or manifests; assert a future outcome;
fabricate chronology; or bind players to the montage. An authorized foreshadowing
disclosure is a separate committed story action, not an excuse to publish secret
prediction. A trailer is skippable and its speculative content never enters facts.

`df-presentation::plan_bookend` is pure over supplied authorized facts/basis and
returns a bounded candidate plan. Canonical history lookup uses the df-session-owned
MemoryCandidateStore native port with an authorized RecapHistory purpose and
bounded decision/event range; df-persistence returns exact permitted committed
facts/attributed beliefs through the admitted job. Summaries alone cannot authorize
a recap claim. Pure directors cannot read SQL.
The owner rechecks source/range/audience/run after generation, then publishes only
validated complete assets. Lost receipts/deliveries deduplicate by operation/job/
cue identity. Prepared-only/replay misses make zero live calls; expired/obsolete
bookends use permitted stills/narration/captions, never block session entry or play.

Optional video needs a separate quote/reservation and host-approved allowance.
The outline's 8–15 seconds is a candidate playtest duration, not a mandatory bill.
Bookend art/music/voice uses pinned canonical packs; paid waste and unused clips
remain billed/reconciled under existing unknown-outcome rules. End-of-session jobs
need explicit campaign scope/expiry so closing a browser does not leave orphan work.

`ExportGrant` is an explicit consented request with audience/recipient scope,
permitted fact/media range, capture/contributor rights, attribution/license,
source/access versions, expiry/revocation policy and an export identity. Already
downloaded media cannot be reliably retracted; the grant discloses that boundary
and restricts prospective access instead of promising secret erasure. Sharing is
disabled by default;
the normal private recap is not publicly shareable merely because it has a hash.
G03/G04/G08/G10 and F46-MODEL select the typed operator/host export API/port and
enforceable revocation limitations before dispatch. This plan creates no unnumbered
public RPC. No provider, browser or agent publishes a recap to another service.

## Critical-event cinematic escalation

`CriticalCueEligibility` references a committed source-valid resolution/event,
importance/emotional context permitted to the audience, ready identity assets,
cue frequency/fatigue, duration/deadline and budget policy. A natural 20 is only a
source-specific rule input/result; it never makes every check automatically succeed
or reverses a committed failure. A qualifying event may select a short shot,
camera/duck/impact cue or a prepared still/narration fallback.

Presentation can slow cosmetic animation locally while state/input continue.
If a genuine game pause is desired, it requires existing authorized host/session
pause semantics and current pending reaction/source timer policy. A video may not
silently freeze rules resources, hide legal actions, reorder reaction windows or
delay input for all players. Skip/reduced-motion/mute preserves the same outcome.
One-shot identity and epoch stop repeated snapshots/reconnect from replaying a kill.

## Location and item continuity

`SceneIdentityRevision` binds stable location identity, source world-change facts,
approved visual layer diff, geometry revision and canonical pack. Weather, burnt
stalls/barricades/refugees appear only from committed world state; if geometry or
visibility changes, rules validate it before rendering. Revisit selects the correct
version while old reference assets remain immutable. A late new image cannot revert
the scene, leak hidden damage or retain obsolete collision/lighting rules.

`ItemOrigin` links the item instance to committed award/event and legal definition,
with name/lore and appearance/voice/SFX revisions. Upgrades create source-compatible
definition or explicit custom-rules revisions under [Generated content](generated-content.md).
Visual evolution never changes statistics or spends rewards twice. Identity across
portrait/map/still/video/audio uses the same canonical references, matching fallback
and bounded cache invalidation; no one generator invents the next world version.

## Acceptance

F46-MODEL/DELIVER/ACCEPT add recap/trailer selection, budgeted jobs and mounted
playback using existing journal/assets/audio. F15-CRITICAL-ACCEPT and
F35-CONTINUITY-ACCEPT extend canonical implementations. Check private/public/member
recap differences, false-belief attribution, spoiler noninterference, skipped clips,
no consent/no export, rights conflicts, cancel/expiry/stale results, replay misses,
source migration, late assets and duplicate cues. Verify zero invented future facts,
no source outcome retcon, no unapproved time pause, and identical legal input with
cinematics disabled. Actual both-role browser/vision/audio review measures recap
recall, interruption/fatigue, frame/audio timing, accepted cost and prefetch waste;
planning examples do not prove delight or customer willingness to pay.
